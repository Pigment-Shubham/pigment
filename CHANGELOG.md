# Changelog

## 3.8.1
- Screensaver can start by itself: open `#saver-play` on the site, or use the single file `pigment-screensaver.html`, to run it as a Mac screensaver with a free helper app.

## 3.8.0
- New Screensaver tab: plays your moving wallpapers full screen and fades from one to the next. Choose vibes, how often it changes, palette and an optional clock that drifts around the screen. Keeps the screen awake where the browser allows it.

## 3.7.0
- Works offline on the GitHub site: after one visit it opens without internet.
- Backup reminder on the Saved page when you have 3 or more saves and no backup in 2 weeks.

## 3.6.3
- Removed the Apple style wallpapers (Command Key, Glass Command, Spotlight Bar, Dynamic Pill, Activity Rings, Siri Orb).

## 3.6.2
- Desktop wallpapers: the strip at the top for the menu bar now continues the wallpaper's own colour instead of fading to a fixed colour, so dark wallpapers get a dark top and no white fade.
- Pill wallpapers leave more room at the top on desktop.

## 3.6.1
- New Pills vibe: Pill Row, Pill Wave, Pill Fan and Pill Night.

## 3.6.0
- Search box in Browse: type words like dark, calm, moon, apple or warm.
- Favourite palette: show every style in one palette you choose.
- Today's pick has a Download button.
- Share card: a ready-to-post image of your wallpaper in a phone frame.

## 3.5.1
- New Apple vibe with 6 one-focus wallpapers: Command Key, Glass Command, Spotlight Bar, Dynamic Pill, Activity Rings and Siri Orb.

## 3.5.0
- 10 new wallpapers: Liquid Metal, Lava Lamp, Weather Sky (follows your real time of day), Tarot Sun, Stained Glass, Rainy Window, Campfire, Moon Phase (tonight's real phase), Autumn Leaves and Pixel Dusk.
- Cards have a one-tap download button.
- Surprise me is now a full-screen shuffle: swipe or use the arrow keys to skip or keep, Enter to open.
- Phone preview has a before/after switch and a live lock-screen clock.
- Saved wallpapers can be sorted into collections.
- Compare palettes slider in the wallpaper viewer.
- First-visit tour and a keyboard shortcuts list (press ?).
- Your Look settings and collections are included in profile backup and restore, so they follow you to other devices.
- Phone layout has a bottom tab bar.

## 3.4.1
- Browse filters are clearer: the two rows are now labelled "Filter by vibe" and "Pick a style", with a divider between them and clearer "All vibes" and "All styles" buttons.

## 3.4.0
- The assistant can now invent its own one-off wallpapers: say "invent a sunset over the sea" and it builds a layered composition (sky, glow, sun or moon, stars, waves, mountains, rings, rays). With Claude connected it designs from scratch for your exact idea.
- New "Look" button in the top bar: Auto, Light or Dark theme, a background colour, and an optional slowly moving background.
- New "Set up" page with steps for iPhone, Mac and Windows.
- Cleaner home screen (removed the scrolling strip and the colour row).
- Fixed the assistant crashing on some requests after styles were removed.

## 3.3.1
- Removed the "Show every colour" switch. Browse shows one look per style; picking a style still shows its colours.

## 3.3.0
- Trimmed from 81 to 39 styles (975 wallpapers) to keep the collection focused. Removed styles are no longer listed; old saved copies of them are simply hidden.
- Fixed the mood buttons (Focus, Sleep, Cozy, Party), which sometimes failed to open a wallpaper.

## 3.2.1
- Browse now shows one look per style (81) instead of every colour version. A "Show every colour" switch brings back all 2,025, and picking a style still shows all its colours.

## 3.2.0
- 9 new live scenes: Storm Hills, Snowfall, Pixel Dusk, Isometric City, Rose Window (stained glass), Tarot Sun, Star Trails, Rainy Window, Grass Field. Now 81 styles and 2,025 wallpapers.

## 3.1.1
- Detail slider now changes all Designer styles (softness, count of shapes).

## 3.1.0
- 8 designer gradient styles (Mesh Gradient, Liquid Satin, Glow Orb, Soft Waves, Prism Glass, Petal Bloom, Soft Spheres, Aura Rings) and a Designer filter. 1,800 wallpapers.
- "© Made By Shubham Patel" credit beside the logo on every page; top bar wraps on phones.

## 3.0.0
- 8 cinematic live scenes (Moonlit Lake, Neon City, Sunset Sea, Blossom Night, Fireworks, Warp Speed, Deep Sea, Ink Smoke) and a Scenes filter.
- Today's pick moved to the top of the home screen with its caption above the picture.

## 2.x

- Frame-exact 16-second loop videos (WebCodecs + built-in MP4 writer), packs (zip), text overlay, own-photo colours, seasonal palettes, favourites, moods, Surprise me, share links, light + dark pairs.
