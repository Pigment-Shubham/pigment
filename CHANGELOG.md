# Changelog

## 3.3.1
- Removed the "Show every colour" switch. Browse shows one look per style; picking a style still shows its colours.

## 3.3.0
- Trimmed from 81 to 39 styles (975 wallpapers) to keep the collection focused. Removed styles are no longer listed; old saved copies of them are simply hidden.
- Fixed the mood buttons (Focus, Sleep, Cozy, Party), which sometimes failed to open a wallpaper.

## 3.2.1
- Browse now shows one look per style (81) instead of every colour version. A "Show every colour" switch brings back all 2,025, and picking a style still shows all its colours.

## 3.2.0
- 9 new live scenes: Storm Hills, Snowfall, Pixel Dusk, Isometric City, Rose Window (stained glass), Tarot Sun, Star Trails, Rainy Window, Grass Field. Now 81 styles and 2,025 wallpapers.

## 3.1.1
- Detail slider now changes all Designer styles (softness, count of shapes).

## 3.1.0
- 8 designer gradient styles (Mesh Gradient, Liquid Satin, Glow Orb, Soft Waves, Prism Glass, Petal Bloom, Soft Spheres, Aura Rings) and a Designer filter. 1,800 wallpapers.
- "© Made By Shubham Patel" credit beside the logo on every page; top bar wraps on phones.

## 3.0.0
- 8 cinematic live scenes (Moonlit Lake, Neon City, Sunset Sea, Blossom Night, Fireworks, Warp Speed, Deep Sea, Ink Smoke) and a Scenes filter.
- Today's pick moved to the top of the home screen with its caption above the picture.

## 2.x
- Aesthetic styles (Riso, Blueprint, Zen Ink, Memphis, HUD, Film Leak, Terrazzo, Art Deco), premium styles, music-reactive styles, Pigment brand wallpapers, vibe filter.
- Frame-exact 16-second loop videos (WebCodecs + built-in MP4 writer), packs (zip), text overlay, own-photo colours, seasonal palettes, favourites, moods, Surprise me, share links, light + dark pairs.

## 1.x
- First versions: procedural wallpaper styles, Studio, 4K and iPhone downloads, profile with saved wallpapers, installable app.
